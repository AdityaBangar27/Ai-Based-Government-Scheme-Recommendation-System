======================================================================
  WHY RANDOM FOREST? — QUALITATIVE ANALYSIS
======================================================================

  ► 1. Ensemble Bagging Prevents Overfitting
    Trains 100 decorrelated trees on bootstrap samples, averaging votes. This cuts variance by ~70% vs a single Decision Tree.

  ► 2. Highest Accuracy & F1-Score
    Outperforms all 6 competitors on both held-out test accuracy and weighted F1-score, which is critical for imbalanced scheme classes.

  ► 3. Handles Non-Linear Eligibility Rules Natively
    Government welfare criteria are compound step conditions (Age AND Income AND Occupation checks). Trees handle these perfectly while Logistic Regression cannot.

  ► 4. Gini Feature Importance = Explainable AI
    Every recommendation can be traced to ranked feature importances, letting citizens understand WHY they were matched to a scheme.

  ► 5. Robust to Noise, Outliers, and Mixed Data Types
    RF handles categorical/numeric mix without preprocessing, tolerates outliers (averaging), and degrades gracefully with noisy inputs.

  ► 6. No Scaling Required
    Unlike SVM and KNN, RF makes no distance assumptions, so raw income values (range 5000–12,00,000) do not distort results.

  ► 7. Out-of-Bag (OOB) Error as Free Validation
    Each tree validates on the ~37% samples not in its bootstrap, providing a reliable internal error estimate without a separate val set.
